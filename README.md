**Ember Pact** is an original card duel: you are the Pactbound, facing the Ember
Warden across the table. Twenty life each, one card a round, the pact goes to
the last ember standing.

## How to play

- Each round you and the Warden play one card face down, then reveal together.
- **Element wheel:** Flame beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Flame.
  Stone is neutral. Advantage is +3 power.
- **Higher total wins the trick.** Damage equals the difference. Equal power is
  a Clash — no damage.
- **Special cards:** Mend heals 5 · Ward blocks all damage next round · Surge
  doubles your power this round · Quake deals 3 direct damage, no trick.
- **Ember Chain:** win 3 tricks in a row and the third deals +2 bonus damage.
- **Read the Warden:** its mood line before you commit hints at the element it
  will play — counter it with the wheel.
- Your hand holds 5 cards; you draw 1 each round. The discard pile stays
  visible with a count per element, so you can count what is gone.

## Controls

- Click a card, or press **1–5** to play it
- **Enter** to start · **R** to restart after a duel · **M** to mute
- Sound is synthesized WebAudio — no audio files.

## Run it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or serve the `game/` folder with
any static server. No build step, no network requests.

Demo states for screenshots: `game/index.html?demo=title`,
`?demo=table`, `?demo=win`, `?demo=loss`.
