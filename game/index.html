A DUEL OF EMBERS

EMBER PACT

You are the Pactbound. The Ember Warden waits across the table.
Twenty life each. One card a round. The pact goes to the last ember standing.

How to play

  • Each round you and the Warden play one card face down, then reveal together.
  • Element wheel: ▲ Flame beats Thorn · ✦ Thorn beats Tide · ● Tide beats Flame · ◆ Stone is neutral. Advantage is +3 power.
  • Higher total wins the trick. Damage equals the difference. Equal power is a Clash — no damage.
  • Specials: Mend heals 5 · Ward blocks all damage next round · Surge doubles your power this round · Quake deals 3 direct damage, no trick.
  • Ember Chain: win 3 tricks in a row and the third deals +2 bonus damage.
  • Read the Warden: its mood line before you commit hints at the element it will play.
  • Controls: click a card or press 1–5 · Enter to start · R to restart · M to mute.
